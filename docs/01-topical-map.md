# Hindi with Neelam: topical map and search-intent clusters

Prepared 13 November 2026. Scope: online Hindi lessons from India for families in the USA and Australia. The uploaded brief is research, not an instruction to enter other countries, offer exam tutoring, install extensions, or adopt its suggested prices.

## Positioning and entity

Primary entity: Neelam, the same India-based teacher as English with Neelam. New brand: Hindi with Neelam. Audience: parents seeking family connection through Hindi, heritage learners who understand more than they speak, and beginners. Audience age, Hindi-specific credentials, fees and timetable require owner confirmation. Offer enquiries, not instant purchases or unverified availability.
